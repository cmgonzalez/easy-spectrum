# Play Store listing — English (United States) en-US (default language)

## Easy Spectrum (Free) — cl.easysoft.easyspectrum

**Name (≤30):** Easy Spectrum: ZX Emulator

**Short description (≤80):** ZX Spectrum 48K, 128K and +3 emulator with keyboard, gamepad and fast load.

**Full description:**

Bring the ZX Spectrum back to life on your phone. Easy Spectrum is a simple, accessible emulator with big buttons, readable text and the classic Spectrum look: black and rainbow.

MODELS
• ZX Spectrum 16K, 48K, 128K, +2, +2A and +3
• ZX Spectrum Next for .nex files

FORMATS
• Tapes: .tap, .tzx and .csw
• Snapshots: .z80, .sna (48K and 128K) and .szx
• +3 disks: .dsk
• Also inside .zip files

LOADING AND TAPES
• Auto-load: pick a game and it starts by itself
• Fast tape loading up to 50× speed, even with custom loaders (a 3½-minute tape loads in about 40 seconds)
• Tape manager: block list, play, pause, stop, previous/next block, eject
• Record your own BASIC programs with SAVE to a new tape

CONTROLS
• Multi-touch on-screen ZX keyboard with CAPS SHIFT and SYMBOL SHIFT
• Gamepad with 8-way D-pad and 1 to 4 buttons (fire plus keys of your choice), optional Select/Start
• Kempston, Sinclair 1 and 2, Cursor or custom keys
• Gamepad settings saved per game
• Kempston mouse and haptic feedback

PICTURE AND SOUND
• Video modes: sharp, smooth, rounded corners, monitor and CRT TV with scanlines and curvature
• Gigascreen and ULAplus
• Beeper and AY chip sound in stereo

YOUR LIBRARY
• Import games from your files or open them from other apps (email, WhatsApp, Telegram, browser)
• Thumbnails with each game's loading screen
• Game details (title, year, publisher, genre) from ZXDB

Available in English, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian.

Prefer no ads? Try Easy Spectrum Pro.

Easy Spectrum does not include games: load the files you own. The Spectrum ROMs are included with permission from Amstrad plc. Easy Spectrum is not affiliated with Sinclair, Amstrad or Sky.
